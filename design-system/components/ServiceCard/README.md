# ServiceCard

A card linking to one inspection service page, with a short summary and up to five scope items.

Provide `title`, `summary` (one sentence on the problem it solves), `items` (scope lines from the approved service description), optional `code`, and `href`. Lay cards out in `uvg-grid` in rows of three on desktop. Keep summaries under 120 characters so cards in a row stay level. Do not add stock icons or images inside the card.
